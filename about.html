About

A small studio
in Wigan.

AEAN Studios is me, Emma Winnard, a graphic designer, illustrator and art director based in Wigan. I design websites and social media for small businesses, and book covers and YouTube thumbnails for authors and creators, and I work on every project myself, from the first message to the final files.

I'd rather do a few things carefully than everything quickly.

Most of my clients are small businesses who want a website and a social feed that look as good as the work they do, and independent authors and YouTubers who need a cover or a thumbnail that makes someone stop and look. I also design logos and brand identities, and shoot brand photography around Wigan when a project needs it.

Because it's just me, you deal with the person doing the work from start to finish. I read every brief myself, reply within a day or so, and explain the technical side in plain English, so you're never left guessing what you're paying for or how to use it once it's done.

At the moment I'm also helping a children's author with the outreach side of her book series, which is something I'm hoping to offer more authors in the future.

The name

AEAN Studios takes its name from Aeaea, the island of Circe.

In myth, Aeaea is a place apart, remote and cultivated and charged with transformation, where a woman stands in her own power surrounded by loyal creatures, living gardens and the steady force of her craft.

I named the studio after it because that balance of instinct and discipline is how I like to work. Ideas are allowed to start out wild, and then they're shaped until every part of the design has a reason to be there.

The mark

The lion at the heart of the studio.

The lion stands for loyalty, strength and a calm kind of confidence, which is what I want clients to feel when they work with me. It sits at the centre of the studio's seal, a mark designed to look as good pressed into paper as it does on a screen.

How a project runs

From first message
to finished work.

Listen

We start with a conversation about your business, your customers and what you need the work to do, and I ask a lot of questions before anything gets designed.

Shape

I sketch out directions and build the bones of the design, from the palette and type to the layout, and show you where it's heading early so there are no surprises.

Refine

Your feedback guides each round of changes, and I work through the small details, from the spacing to the kerning, until every part has earned its place.

Hand over

You get the finished files or the live website, set up and explained, so you know exactly how to use what you've paid for.
