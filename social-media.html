Why It Works

People remember
the story behind the brand.

People buy from people. A feed that shows why a business exists, who's behind it and what they actually believe reads differently to one built from a generic content calendar. Consistency matters too. Most accounts don't stall because the content isn't good, they stall because posting stops being regular and nobody's keeping the plan moving. A single filming day removes that problem for months at a time.

Frequently Asked

A few things
worth knowing.

What does the social media management service include?

An audit, a content plan, one filming day covering an agreed period of one to three months, then editing, captions and scheduling delivered within a week. That's the Production Sprint. Full Management adds ongoing replies, outreach and community engagement on top, week to week between shoots.

Do you film the content, or do I need to provide it?

Content is filmed and photographed from scratch on the agreed day, built around your brand's existing visual identity. It's genuinely shot content, not stock footage or AI-generated material. If you already have your own photography or video, that can be worked into the plan too.

Which platforms do you cover?

Plans are built around wherever your audience actually is, rather than posting everywhere by default. Most projects centre on Instagram, with Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube and others added when they genuinely fit your brand and audience. A Google Business Profile can also be kept current as part of the plan.

How much does it cost?

Every plan is quoted individually after an initial conversation about your goals, where you're starting from, and which tier fits. Get in touch for a tailored quote.

Where is AEAN Studios based?

The studio is based in the UK and works with clients remotely, wherever they're based.

Ready to hand over the feed?

Tell me about your brand and where you'd like your presence to be in six months. I'll take it from there.
