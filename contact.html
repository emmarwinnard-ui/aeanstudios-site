Before You Write

A few things
worth knowing.

What do you work on?

Websites and social media for small businesses, and book covers and YouTube thumbnails for authors and creators. I also design logos and brand identities and shoot brand photography around Wigan.

How does a project start?

With a conversation. Send an enquiry, and I'll reply with questions about your brand, your audience and what "done well" looks like to you before any quote is given.

Where are you based?

Wigan, in Greater Manchester. I work in person with clients around Wigan and the North West, and remotely with clients anywhere else in the UK and abroad.
