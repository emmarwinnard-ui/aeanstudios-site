Construction Note

Every mark earns
its presence.

A logo is a promise of provenance: proof of where something came from and who stands behind it. AEAN's own mark borrows the same instinct that built the lion: nothing decorative, only what holds weight at any size.

Every identity on this page follows that same discipline: restraint first, ornament only where it earns its place.
